# Prep Product Capabilities

> Generated from Harness canonical knowledge. Do not edit this file directly.

## Purpose

Decompose accepted product intent into observable product capabilities without selecting domain ownership, interface form, algorithms, persistence, or implementation technology.

## Requirements

### REQ-CAP-TARGET

The product shall let the learner create, inspect, and revise an active preparation target while preserving explicit uncertainty and distinguishing target refinement from learner-state change.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-TARGET-REFINEMENT

Rationale:

Target definition must remain observable and revisable.

### REQ-CAP-TARGET-PURPOSE

The product shall let the learner distinguish related target purposes and inspect which requirements belong to each without automatically inheriting requirements between them.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-TARGET-PURPOSE

Rationale:

Related role, interview, certification, or other targets may overlap without being equivalent.

### REQ-CAP-PERFORMANCE-REQUIREMENT

The product shall let the learner inspect what performance a target requires, including material conditions or constraints and acceptable quality, separately from topic coverage.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-CAPABILITY-PERFORMANCE

Rationale:

Readiness must be anchored in required performance rather than subject exposure alone.

### REQ-CAP-EVIDENCE-CONTEXT

The product shall accept or record capability-relevant observations with enough performance context, provenance, target relevance, and time information to keep the observation distinguishable from conclusions drawn from it.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-STATE-EVIDENCE
- REQ-EVIDENCE-JUSTIFICATION
- REQ-PERFORMANCE-EVIDENCE
- REQ-DURABLE-TRANSFER

Rationale:

Evidence-backed state and transfer judgments require contextualized observations rather than context-free success/failure markers.

### REQ-CAP-STATE

The product shall derive or present target-relative learner state so demonstrated, challenged, and unknown capability can be distinguished while preserving material limits from evidence coverage, context, and age.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-STATE-EVIDENCE

Rationale:

Current state is useful only when uncertainty and evidence limitations remain visible.

### REQ-CAP-EVIDENCE-JUSTIFICATION

The product shall let the learner inspect why particular observations support, challenge, or fail to establish a capability conclusion.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-EVIDENCE-JUSTIFICATION

Rationale:

Readiness conclusions must be reviewable rather than opaque assertions.

### REQ-CAP-FOCUS

The product shall support establishing an explicit next preparation focus from target relevance, current evidence, unresolved uncertainty, and available time.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-NEXT-FOCUS

Rationale:

Limited time requires an actionable next-focus capability.

### REQ-CAP-PRACTICE

The product shall support or delegate learning, practice, or diagnostic activity that can generate evidence relevant to the performance required by the active target.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-PERFORMANCE-EVIDENCE

Rationale:

The product must enable evidence-producing preparation without prescribing one study runtime or exercise representation.

### REQ-CAP-DURABLE-TRANSFER

The product shall support judging whether relevant capability remains available over a required time horizon and transfers to target-relevant conditions rather than relying on immediate or narrow success alone.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-DURABLE-TRANSFER

Rationale:

Retention and transfer are observable readiness concerns distinct from one successful attempt.

### REQ-CAP-SUPPORT-FIT

The product shall support selecting or providing learning, practice, or diagnostic opportunities appropriate to the required outcome while keeping support completion distinct from evidence that capability was developed.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-SUPPORT-FIT

Rationale:

Different required performances can need different support forms without making any one mechanism mandatory.

### REQ-CAP-ADAPT

The product shall update target-relative learner state or preparation focus when accepted new evidence changes them and shall revise target understanding separately when new external target information warrants it.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-ADAPTIVE-UPDATE

Rationale:

Learner change and target refinement must remain distinguishable.

### REQ-CAP-BOOTSTRAP

The product shall construct usable preparation support from fragmented or incomplete source material and support incremental introduction or correction of target, capability, knowledge, and evidence-design data without requiring learner-maintained reusable corpus curation.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-LOW-OVERHEAD-BOOTSTRAP

Rationale:

Preparation must be able to start before complete structured support data exists.

### REQ-CAP-KNOWLEDGE-OVERVIEW

The product shall provide an inspectable high-level overview of important concepts within the currently relevant subject scope and how that scope fits together.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-KNOWLEDGE-ORIENTATION

Rationale:

Subject orientation must remain available without committing to a graph or other presentation technique.

### REQ-CAP-KNOWLEDGE-RELATIONSHIPS

The product shall let the learner inspect meaningful relationships among relevant concepts while preserving the meaning of each relationship rather than exposing generic connectivity only.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-KNOWLEDGE-RELATIONSHIPS

Rationale:

Relationship semantics must remain observable without selecting a concrete relation taxonomy here.

### REQ-CAP-KNOWLEDGE-SCOPE

The product shall let the learner narrow and expand the considered subject-knowledge scope using meaningful criteria relevant to the active preparation purpose.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-KNOWLEDGE-SCOPE

Rationale:

Scope control is distinct from the presentation mechanism used to express it.

### REQ-CAP-KNOWLEDGE-DEPTH

The product shall let the learner move between high-level orientation and deeper knowledge detail while retaining enough surrounding conceptual context to stay oriented.

Status: ACCEPTED

Sources:

- REQ-KNOWLEDGE-DEPTH

Rationale:

Progressive depth does not require a specific hierarchy, graph layout, or navigation pattern.

## Non-goals

- Define Capability, CapabilitySpecification, PerformanceExpectation, EvidencePattern, EvidentialWarrant, AssessmentDesign, LearningMaterial, or any other domain entity.
- Require a curator/operator role as part of the learner product flow.
- Require reusable internal target/capability/knowledge/evidence data to be maintained by the learner.
- Require graph exploration, a 2D or 3D graph, cards, questions, spaced repetition, or any other specific learning representation.
- Define target, evidence, learner-state, capability, knowledge, assessment, or relation ownership.
- Define a concrete relation taxonomy, performance taxonomy, evidence-warrant model, or decomposition model.
- Define ranking, scheduling, inference, extraction, generation, or graph-layout algorithms.
- Define import schema, API, persistence, graph database, interface layout, or implementation architecture.
