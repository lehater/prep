# Prep research workspace

The `docs/research/` directory collects noncanonical research about Prep: domain questions, alternative interpretations, counterexamples, bounded experiments and findings. It lets future contributors reuse evidence across conversations and avoid repeating earlier analysis.

Research evidence is distinct from Prep's canonical engineering knowledge. Decisions and approved product/domain semantics are managed by the owning Authority and Harness, not by these notes.
