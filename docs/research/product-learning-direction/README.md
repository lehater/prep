# Product learning direction — research overview

**NONCANONICAL PRODUCT-DISCOVERY RESEARCH.** This topic examines **what the learner does**, what changes when a person learns, and what role Prep could take in supporting that process. It does not select an MVP, an instructional method, a domain ontology, product acceptance criteria, or the next mandatory engineering task.

## Research framing

Primary inquiry: **For which learner goals and learning situations could Prep help a person move from an intended outcome to a usable ability, and which parts of preparation should Prep support directly rather than leave to external tools, teachers and the learner?**

This question deliberately remains open about:
- whether the initial Goal is competence development, an external outcome, or both;
- whether the person needs to acquire, retrieve, understand, apply or demonstrate Knowledge;
- whether Prep navigates and records external activity, hosts active study, coaches applied practice, or combines these;
- whether feedback only records what happened or supports an assessment claim;
- who chooses what to study or practise next.
