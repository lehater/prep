## Key distinction at handoff

Prep's **current accepted product focus** is exploring preparation information and relations, selecting it for external use, and understanding recorded Activity/Results over time. It does **not** presently require automated learning assessment/mastery/readiness, an integrated flashcard trainer, adaptive tutor or prescribed learning path. Such features can be investigated without pretending they are accepted. A distinct Owner-confirmed Goal-first product-direction input exists in [Capability / Goal handoff](../capability-goal-semantics/HANDOFF.md), not yet Product Requirements admission for new observable promises.

Research evidence: [National Academies](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/13398/chapter/6), [Dunlosky et al.](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/publications/journals/pspi/learning-techniques.html), [OECD formative assessment](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/unlocking-high-quality-teaching_f5b82176-en/full-report/using-formative-assessment-and-feedback_189fb6dc.html). Product feature examples and source limits: [landscape scan](landscape-scan-2026-10-12.md).

## No implicit status promotion

Product exploration → evidence-based alternatives → explicit Owner product choice → owning Authority/Product Requirements → routed Harness → impacted downstream domain/application/interface acceptance. A passing experiment and a research recommendation do not make this transition by themselves.
