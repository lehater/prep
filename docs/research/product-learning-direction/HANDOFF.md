## Knowledge versus capability reducibility — bounded research result

[Iteration 02](iteration-02-knowledge-skill-capability-reducibility.md) reviews knowledge-how/knowledge-that, declarative/procedural learning, whole-task integration, EQF and competence-based knowledge space theory. It **does not resolve the philosophical question**. It does show that Prep's current *subject Knowledge* and a claim that a person can *perform* require different evidence, even when assembly/integration strategies themselves are teachable Knowledge. Synthetic design/programming/diagnosis/motor checks explain failure of **naive** K-depth → C mastery inference; they are **not** human validation. Q-PLD-11/12 remain **OPEN**; EX-PLD-05 remains **PROPOSED / NOT RUN**. A choice of a separate Capability projection, any C–K predicate, or personal assessment is **not approved**.

## Key distinction at handoff

Prep's **current accepted product focus** is exploring preparation information and relations, selecting it for external use, and understanding recorded Activity/Results over time. It does **not** presently require automated learning assessment/mastery/readiness, an integrated flashcard trainer, adaptive tutor or prescribed learning path. Such features can be investigated without pretending they are accepted. A distinct Owner-confirmed Goal-first product-direction input exists in [Capability / Goal handoff](../capability-goal-semantics/HANDOFF.md), not yet Product Requirements admission for new observable promises.
