# Open experiments — product learning direction

**Research experiment register (updated 2026-10-13).** This file owns experiment states, hypotheses, falsifiers and outputs. Dated reports own actual evidence; detailed protocols/contract documents own their own methods. The EX-PLD-01 public-trace proxy is not recruited human observation, and no user-value comparison has run. Research tests are independent of Harness and imply no engineering acceptance. Experiments may run in parallel when their results are independently testable; explicitly join compatible outputs before an integrated test.

## EX-PLD-01 — Reconstruct actual recent preparation episodes

- **Status:** ADAPTED ONLINE PUBLIC-TRACE PROXY COMPLETED (2026-10-12; 16 coded first-person forum reports, 7 full-thread / 9 indexed-excerpt); ORIGINAL INTERVIEW / OBSERVED-ACTION VARIANT NOT RUN. [Protocol, source-by-source findings, counterexamples and methodological limits](experiment-01-public-forum-episode-review.md).
- **Question:** What do people actually do and struggle with when moving from a learning outcome to the next useful action? Is the main bottleneck missing content, poor retention, lack of practice/feedback, uncertainty about gaps, or coordination across tools?
- **Sample (not quota):** purposively seek 3–5 contrasting recent episodes, ideally at least one recall-intensive and one application/performance-intensive scenario; preserve non-user availability as a limitation.
- **Freeze before interview:** same prompt ("Walk through a real recent preparation goal: what did you use, how did you decide what was necessary, what did you do, how did you tell whether it worked, what happened next?"), observable artifacts/timestamps where voluntarily available, coding categories for confusion and tool-switching. Do not ask leading questions about desired Prep features.
- **Potential falsifiers:** users already have a reliable next-action process; lack of content or expert feedback dominates instead of organization; a learning action does not start with Capability; goal may be externally determined.
- **Public-trace outcome (not an interview replacement of equal strength):** NAV scope/depth issues appear in concrete reports, but practice/feedback, unreliable interview criteria, card authoring burden, time and usable existing-tool solutions also appear. Categories are purposely sampled, NOT population rates. No Prep-specific demand, observed switching benefit or product success established. Existing Q-PLD-01 remains OPEN and EX-PLD-06 NOT RUN.
- **Stop/evidence for any later interviews:** anonymized episode traces, counterexamples and explicit uncertainty; no automatic Owner scope decision.

## EX-PLD-02 — Same-goal contrast of Prep roles

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN; conditional on EX-PLD-01 or equivalent authentic user need.
- **Question:** Which direct support, if any, meaningfully changes the user's behavior beyond navigation?
- **Common task and data:** same user Goal and subject knowledge, materials and observed exercise failure; compare three deliberately small *paper/mock* interaction descriptions: A external learning organizer, B adds in-product knowledge retrieval/feedback, C adds applied task/feedback guidance. Keep available Knowledge and Activity/Result facts identical; do not give one version exclusive extra hints.
- **Observable outcome:** user can tell what to do next and why, whether they can recover goal context, whether they correctly distinguish coverage from skill deficit; if actually teaching, measure delayed/new-task outcomes separately.
- **Potential falsifiers:** added teaching feature does not change useful decisions, adds friction, or leads to unsupported mastery interpretation; pure organizer remains insufficient to act.
- **Stop/evidence:** task observation, errors, rationale and limitations, not preference polling. Does not approve a product variant.

## EX-PLD-03 — Knowledge-first versus ability-first edge cases

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN.
- **Question:** When does a mandatory Goal→Capability→Knowledge→Material journey misrepresent a learner's real task?
- **Freeze cases:** (1) memorize legal/medical terminology; (2) explain and apply caching tradeoffs; (3) demonstrate a strict physical protocol; (4) attain an external credential through variable routes. Compare the **same meaning** under Knowledge-first, Capability-first and activity-first task descriptions, without inventing universal prerequisites.
- **Potential falsifier:** a case requires fabricating a Capability assertion, or a practical ability involves tacit feedback that cannot be addressed by listing Knowledge.
- **Stop/evidence:** a concise counterexample matrix. Desk representability does not prove real-world preference.

## EX-PLD-04 — Feedback and assessment claim validity

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN; only if an automatic gap-detection proposition is under consideration.
- **Question:** What can responsibly be concluded from a recorded attempt, test answer, self-report or external assessment?
- **Compare:** "attempted exercise, made error E", "learner wants to revisit concept K", "K is missing from selected material", and "learner lacks capability C." For each, record evidence required, valid statement, unwarranted inference and alternative explanation.
- **Potential falsifier:** repeated high performance on a familiar item fails to generalize to novel task; absence of a node in system falsely treated as inability; wrong answer caused by ambiguous prompt rather than lack of knowledge.
- **Stop/evidence:** bounded product-claim contract; **no new mastery model** without explicit product authority and valid assessment design.

## EX-PLD-05 — Does integrated Capability need its own semantics beyond a Knowledge-depth overlay?

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN. [Desk alternatives and counterexamples](iteration-01-knowledge-space-and-applied-capability.md) are **not** user experiments or a selected model.
- **Question:** Can a user distinguish "I have enough coverage/depth on all listed topics" from "I have demonstrated their coordinated application for this particular professional task"?
- **Freeze before execution:** build one small stable Knowledge set (e.g. API, transactions, idempotency, retries), two distinct applied Capabilities (e.g. implement payment intent; explain distributed failure tradeoffs), and two Goals with different constraints. Include a user who can describe individual K's but has failed an integrated task, and a separate unobserved K (unknown, not zero). Describe one technically valid alternative approach with a different K-support set.
- **Compare:** H-A fixed K bundle-depth projection; H-B action/outcome/conditions with conditional K role relations; H-C independent scalar 'K application' depth. Same facts and task prompt; no exclusive hints.
- **Falsifiers:** required K lists imply universal necessity despite alternatives; full individual K coverage incorrectly predicts successful C; an unobserved K is shown as inability; a C assertion loses what activity was actually performed for which Goal; model merely renames execution step ordering as C parts.
- **Measure:** for each case, can one (i) identify the intended C performance, (ii) explain why these K's are selected *under these conditions*, (iii) distinguish what has been observed from what remains unknown, (iv) select a specific next learning or integrative-practice action? Mark logical representability separately from usefulness observed with people.
- **Stop:** compare outputs/counterexamples and report insufficient evidence honestly; do **not** write Harness Domain Model, grant relation authority, infer personal competence, or implement UI before needed.

**EX-PLD-05 research input (2026-10-12):** [Iteration 02](iteration-02-knowledge-skill-capability-reducibility.md) adds checked references for philosophical knowing-how, ACT/procedural learning, 4C/ID, EQF, competence-based knowledge spaces and evidence-centered assessment. It completes a **desk literature / synthetic-case check only**, **not** EX-PLD-05; retain **PROPOSED / NOT RUN**. In the later comparison explicitly distinguish a K topic's explanatory depth from a person's demonstrated performance on task conditions, and allow alternative K-support bundles for the same outcome.

## EX-PLD-06 — Goal → Capability → Knowledge → external theoretical study loop

- **Status:** ORIGINAL HUMAN COMPARISON NOT RUN; **DEFERRED UNTIL A COHERENT INTEGRATED INTERFACE CONCEPT AND WORKING PROTOTYPE ARE READY (Owner correction, 2026-10-13)**. An isolated **synthetic fixture / representability / Anki-text transport preflight** passed on 2026-10-12: [study, counterexamples and limitations](experiment-06-external-study-loop-preflight.md); [fixture and checker](../../../experiments/prep_study_loop/fixture.json). No real participants or measured user value. Protocol reframed following the Owner's 2026-10-12 clarification about user-owned data. Needs a real user/learner with an authentic preparation task and an internally coherent, task-appropriate **supplied test corpus**, **not** independent expert quality certification or completion of EX-PLD-07. [Current research framing](research-framing.md); [historical comparison analysis](iteration-05-owner-knowledge-scope-to-study-material-boundary.md). Earlier [Iterations 03](iteration-03-goal-capability-knowledge-external-retrieval-loop.md) and [04](iteration-04-goal-to-useful-action-market-and-scenarios.md) use conflicting historical variant letters; the labels below control this test.
- **Question:** Given the **same usable user-provided data**, does Prep's Goal/Capability/Knowledge orientation and selected external-study material workflow help the user **understand the represented scope and select/transfer suitable material** better or more efficiently than current tools? Separate value of orientation from value of material selection/export. Do not measure completeness of real-world domain knowledge, expert editorial costs, practical professional ability or employability.
- **Freeze before execution:** one authentic user preparation Goal (a vacancy is an *optional* source of goal constraints, not domain authority); two understandable optional Capability Descriptions; 8–12 reusable Knowledge topics, supplied explanations/relations/conditional scopes; and 8–16 supplied theoretical study items of differing types. Include at least one shared Knowledge item across scopes, two differing Goal-relative objectives and one intentionally off-goal/redundant **but not false** item to test selection. State any unknowns and content assumptions. Verify the **fixture's internal consistency and task usability** against a prewritten answer/task key before testing, without representing that key as an independently certified domain syllabus. Same underlying content, conditions and opportunities to correct errors for every arm. No universal ordinal mastery scale.
- **Comparison labels (task variants only, not product commitments):**
  - **D — credible existing-tools baseline:** participant uses the *same subject information* in a realistic accessible representation plus current notes/roadmaps/AI/NotebookLM/RemNote and, if relevant, Anki or another trainer. Do not artificially deprive baseline of usable tools/content or give it Prep's navigation features without accounting for them.
  - **C — selection/packaging without Goal/Capability Knowledge navigator:** from the same supplied data and external tools, select and transfer study items without the structured perspectives; tests whether packaging alone solves the job. Automated generation is neither required nor presumed.
  - **A — Knowledge orientation alone:** user navigates user-supplied Goal/Capability/Knowledge context and relations to select subjects, without a prepared study-packet feature; external methods remain allowed. Tests orientation independent of export.
  - **B — orientation plus selection/packaging:** A's navigation plus selection/packaging of already available study items for one-way external use. Tests incremental value over A, C and D.
- **Controls:** same content *facts*, task, external tool access, available time/help and precommitted answer/task key; allow realistic baseline formatting while distinguishing the **presentation/workflow treatment** from differing information. Record if AI introduces additional content and treat that difference explicitly, not as Prep's content-quality superiority. Counterbalance order or use between-person design to address learning carryover. Synthetic desk runs can test logical completion but do **not** count as user utility evidence.
- **Primary observations:** (1) *orientation*: accurate explanation of **fixture-represented** scope, conditional links, unknowns and differing study objectives; (2) *selection/transfer*: selection of relevant supplied items, exclusion of off-goal ones, faithful data transfer, import and first appropriate **theoretical** external study action where relevant; (3) *comparative user cost*: elapsed task time, operations, manual rebuilding, errors, abandonment and reasoned participant choices relative to D/C. Preserve orientation-only success for A. Count distinct **tool-induced errors** separately from original input defects. No claims about durable learning, true corpus coverage or actual Capability mastery.
- **Falsifiers:** D performs as well as or better than A/B with less effort; C matches B at lower complexity; added Capability navigation obscures goals or slows correct choices; imported material loses or distorts its source data; user cannot recognize an off-goal item; prepended scope language implies unearned completeness/readiness; export is redundant versus current tools.
- **Deferred add-on (not a fifth arm):** authorized import of external review history/statistics and mapping to selected items. Historical Iteration 03 **P-C** and Iteration 04 **C** labels do not change this test's C. Absence of statistics is not absence of study. This research deferral concerns automatic synchronization only; it does not decide whether to retain Activity/Result behavior in the eventual product.
- **Stop/evidence:** observed comparable task outputs, participant decisions and costs across the arms, observed errors/counterexamples and an Owner options packet. **EX-PLD-07 is a separate optional editorial/content-supply feasibility study, not a gate** to this UX/workflow value experiment. No automatic content generation, guarantee, domain taxonomy, platform integration, competence assessment or Harness approval follows from a positive result.

- **Owner sequencing correction / instrument readiness (2026-10-13):** The previous recommendation to move directly from the minimal A/B/C implementation to comparative usability/value testing is **withdrawn**. That frontend is a technical donor, **not an adequate integrated UX instrument**. First reconcile the full research interaction/information model across **Goals, Capabilities, Knowledge (including typed K–K links where supported) and Materials**; design coherent end-to-end flows; check them as connected clickable concepts with representative data; *then* build a usable browser prototype of those flows and internally verify task completeness, consistent selection/navigation and information parity across future comparison arms. Only after this readiness gate run D/C/A/B with real users. Do not use an obviously deficient frontend to falsify the idea. Do not conflate a Capability Description with measured learner ability. [Existing prototype](../../../experiments/prep_study_loop/prototype/README.md), [workbench candidate](../../../experiments/prep_study_loop/prototype/WORKSPACE_LAYOUT_CANDIDATE.md), [Owner correction/checkpoint](interface-exploration-checkpoint-2026-10-13.md). Related [Q-PLD-16](open-questions.md) and [EX-PLD-09](open-experiments.md#ex-pld-09--integrated-prep-concept-and-end-to-end-interaction-check).

## EX-PLD-07 — Editorial feasibility of a trustworthy goal → knowledge scope → practice packet

- **Status:** ORIGINAL TWO-INDEPENDENT-REVIEWER EXPERIMENT NOT RUN; NON-PREREQUISITE FOR EX-PLD-06 UNDER CURRENT OWNER FRAMING. A bounded **single-author vacancy/source/packet desk preflight** was completed on 2026-10-12: [trace matrix, ten items, unsafe counterexamples and unmeasured review-cost assumptions](experiment-07-squire-editorial-feasibility-preflight.md). No reviewer agreement, independently validated material, timed editorial cost or learner outcomes. Motivated by the desk market and real vacancy comparison in [Iteration 04](iteration-04-goal-to-useful-action-market-and-scenarios.md), not an additional observed user study or an implementation commitment.
- **Scope under current framing:** optional **upstream content curation/editorial economics** question. It does not evaluate whether a user-owned data tool is useful given a fit-for-task dataset; a vacancy is only one possible input. Run this original protocol only if content supply/quality assurance becomes an explicit research concern.
- **Question (original optional protocol):** Can a useful and honest study scope and fit-for-objective practice packet be assembled from one *specific* ambiguous IT vacancy at a sustainable human-review cost, preserving unknowns and conditional Knowledge relevance?
- **Freeze before execution:** use the same publicly accessible vacancy text (e.g. SQUIRE payments-backend role, snapshot/date), two independently working subject reviewers, same goal definition, two applied outcome descriptions, ~10 relevant Knowledge topics, and ~10 prompts/tasks of mixed types. Record source assumptions; declare interviews/experience/production constraints beyond cards. Hold the input and review rubric fixed when comparing output.
- **Measure:** reviewer agreement/disagreement over topic necessity versus plausible relevance, unsupported claims, quality/fit of every practice item, estimated authoring/review/maintenance effort and which output can be used in Anki versus requires external applied task feedback.
- **Falsifiers:** experts cannot agree without inventing employer criteria; the 'complete' scope is misleading; too much editorial time per new goal; reliable recall cards fail to support the intended applied objective; a simpler manual/AI workflow is already sufficient.
- **Stop:** report quality and cost with counterexamples; no automatic adoption of authoring/source-verification Product Requirements or AI content pipeline. A synthetic desk matrix is **not** execution of this reviewer study.

## EX-PLD-08 — Knowledge graph representation: added value and density

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN; **CONDITIONAL, NOT A GATE for EX-PLD-06**. The 2026-10-13 in-chat interactive layout checks supplied Owner design input, **not observed user evidence**. Previous 3D technical stress work is donor evidence only, not EX-PLD-08.
- **Question:** For actual orientation/sensemaking on a realistic user-supplied **typed, directed Knowledge–Knowledge** network, does a visual graph improve interpretation of neighbors, relation meanings and bounded paths over a credible search/table/typed-relations/detail interface? If yes, is 2D sufficient or does 3D supply further justified value?
- **Conditions (if reopened):** R0 = good nonspatial list/search/typed relation table/detail; R1 = same semantic information + 2D graph; R2 = same semantic information + 3D graph. Matched representative K–K data and task bundles, controlled relation meaning/labels and input fidelity; record graph density, viewport/device, layout and viewpoint where applicable. Do not use the EX-PLD-06 fixture as a supposedly realistic K–K graph: it does not define such relations. Use authentic task context and actual participants; balance learning/order effects.
- **Observe:** correctness explaining link types and directions, neighbor/path reconstruction, time/effort, orientation loss and recovery, readability under density, comprehension after the view is hidden; distinguish task-completion outcome from technical FPS and aesthetic preference.
- **Falsifiers:** graph produces equal or worse comprehension / orientation at extra interaction cost; dense graph becomes illegible compared with table; 3D increases occlusion/control overhead without stronger results than 2D; useful tasks succeed with a small local working set and no global graph.
- **Stop:** report task-conditional choice (R0, R1, R2 or optional graph), limitations and counterexamples. Do **not** select a production renderer or infer graph efficacy from [historical 60/250/1000 synthetic Storybook tiers](https://github.com/lehater/prep/tree/main/experiments/knowledge_representation). Question [Q-PLD-14](open-questions.md); [checkpoint](interface-exploration-checkpoint-2026-10-13.md).

## EX-PLD-09 — Integrated Prep concept and end-to-end interaction check

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN; **NEXT AFTER EX-PLD-10 / EX-PLD-11 COMPATIBILITY CHECK** (Owner sequencing clarification, 2026-10-13). A prerequisite for a meaningful EX-PLD-06 comparison instrument, not a substitute for later human comparison or engineering acceptance.
- **Question:** Can a coherent, understandable desktop Prep workspace let a user traverse and work with **Goals, Capability Descriptions, Knowledge, Materials** and their context-sensitive relationships end-to-end, without misleading hierarchy/necessity/competence claims or disconnected panel behaviors?
- **Inputs / controlled conditions:** use the **reviewed model/fixture from EX-PLD-10 and view contract from EX-PLD-11**, after their compatibility review, with all four sections, meaningful K–K relations and C–K objectives where applicable, reused object identities, multiple contextual scopes, heterogeneous materials and incomplete/ambiguous cases. Mark hypothetical types and unverified semantics; do not invent canonical relations. Existing four-panel [L0/L1 + coarse L2 layout](../../../experiments/prep_study_loop/prototype/WORKSPACE_LAYOUT_CANDIDATE.md) is an input, not the only candidate.
- **Bounded concept scenarios:** choose a Goal; inspect scoped Capabilities/Knowledge; follow an existing relation; recognize a shared Knowledge with differing objectives; inspect/select relevant Materials; adjust/clear scope; recover previous context; use synchronized table and graph selection *when a graph applies*. Show what is missing/uncertain without claiming failure or mastery.
- **Observe / falsifiers (formative walkthrough, not EX-PLD-06 efficacy evidence):** a task can/cannot be completed through connected clickable screens; losing current context unexpectedly; inconsistent entity IDs/selections; hidden implicit filtering; dangling transitions; unusable amount of navigation; insufficient cues for relation meaning; an apparently necessary graph with no backed semantic links. Document alternative UI/model assumptions where data do not decide.
- **Completion for the concept phase:** Owner review of one coherent end-to-end interactive concept; account for all four sections, relations and the most important transitions; list deferred details and unresolved semantic choices separately. Then implement a browser prototype faithful to the reviewed concept and verify its usability/task paths before the later EX-PLD-06 comparative protocol. Chat clickthroughs are not evidence of user value. No requirement to finish full production ontology, performance engineering or certify the corpus.
- **References:** [Q-PLD-16](open-questions.md), [research framing](research-framing.md), [concept discussion / correction](interface-exploration-checkpoint-2026-10-13.md). EX-PLD-08 (2D/3D benefit) is independent/conditional, not a prerequisite for an integrated conceptual walkthrough.
