# Open experiments — product learning direction

**NONCANONICAL RESEARCH / PROPOSALS (2026-10-12).** An adapted **EX-PLD-01 public-forum self-report proxy** has been completed; **no recruited human interviews, observed user tasks, usability observations, measured learning outcomes or product-comparison tests** have been performed. These are not Harness tests, implementation tasks, approved UX scope or release gates. Perform only a check that addresses a live uncertainty; no required global execution order.

## EX-PLD-01 — Reconstruct actual recent preparation episodes

- **Status:** ADAPTED ONLINE PUBLIC-TRACE PROXY COMPLETED (2026-10-12; 16 coded first-person forum reports, 7 full-thread / 9 indexed-excerpt); ORIGINAL INTERVIEW / OBSERVED-ACTION VARIANT NOT RUN. [Protocol, source-by-source findings, counterexamples and methodological limits](experiment-01-public-forum-episode-review.md).
- **Question:** What do people actually do and struggle with when moving from a learning outcome to the next useful action? Is the main bottleneck missing content, poor retention, lack of practice/feedback, uncertainty about gaps, or coordination across tools?
- **Sample (not quota):** purposively seek 3–5 contrasting recent episodes, ideally at least one recall-intensive and one application/performance-intensive scenario; preserve non-user availability as a limitation.
- **Freeze before interview:** same prompt ("Walk through a real recent preparation goal: what did you use, how did you decide what was necessary, what did you do, how did you tell whether it worked, what happened next?"), observable artifacts/timestamps where voluntarily available, coding categories for confusion and tool-switching. Do not ask leading questions about desired Prep features.
- **Potential falsifiers:** users already have a reliable next-action process; lack of content or expert feedback dominates instead of organization; a learning action does not start with Capability; goal may be externally determined.
- **Public-trace outcome (not an interview replacement of equal strength):** NAV scope/depth issues appear in concrete reports, but practice/feedback, unreliable interview criteria, card authoring burden, time and usable existing-tool solutions also appear. Categories are purposely sampled, NOT population rates. No Prep-specific demand, observed switching benefit or product success established. Existing Q-PLD-01 remains OPEN and EX-PLD-06 NOT RUN.
- **Stop/evidence for any later interviews:** anonymized episode traces, counterexamples and explicit uncertainty; no automatic Owner scope decision.

## EX-PLD-02 — Same-goal contrast of Prep roles

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN; conditional on EX-PLD-01 or equivalent authentic user need.
- **Question:** Which direct support, if any, meaningfully changes the user's behavior beyond navigation?
- **Common task and data:** same user Goal and subject knowledge, materials and observed exercise failure; compare three deliberately small *paper/mock* interaction descriptions: A external learning organizer, B adds in-product knowledge retrieval/feedback, C adds applied task/feedback guidance. Keep available Knowledge and Activity/Result facts identical; do not give one version exclusive extra hints.
- **Observable outcome:** user can tell what to do next and why, whether they can recover goal context, whether they correctly distinguish coverage from skill deficit; if actually teaching, measure delayed/new-task outcomes separately.
- **Potential falsifiers:** added teaching feature does not change useful decisions, adds friction, or leads to unsupported mastery interpretation; pure organizer remains insufficient to act.
- **Stop/evidence:** task observation, errors, rationale and limitations, not preference polling. Does not approve a product variant.

## EX-PLD-03 — Knowledge-first versus ability-first edge cases

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN.
- **Question:** When does a mandatory Goal→Capability→Knowledge→Material journey misrepresent a learner's real task?
- **Freeze cases:** (1) memorize legal/medical terminology; (2) explain and apply caching tradeoffs; (3) demonstrate a strict physical protocol; (4) attain an external credential through variable routes. Compare the **same meaning** under Knowledge-first, Capability-first and activity-first task descriptions, without inventing universal prerequisites.
- **Potential falsifier:** a case requires fabricating a Capability assertion, or a practical ability involves tacit feedback that cannot be addressed by listing Knowledge.
- **Stop/evidence:** a concise counterexample matrix. Desk representability does not prove real-world preference.

## EX-PLD-04 — Feedback and assessment claim validity

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN; only if an automatic gap-detection proposition is under consideration.
- **Question:** What can responsibly be concluded from a recorded attempt, test answer, self-report or external assessment?
- **Compare:** "attempted exercise, made error E", "learner wants to revisit concept K", "K is missing from selected material", and "learner lacks capability C." For each, record evidence required, valid statement, unwarranted inference and alternative explanation.
- **Potential falsifier:** repeated high performance on a familiar item fails to generalize to novel task; absence of a node in system falsely treated as inability; wrong answer caused by ambiguous prompt rather than lack of knowledge.
- **Stop/evidence:** bounded product-claim contract; **no new mastery model** without explicit product authority and valid assessment design.

## EX-PLD-05 — Does integrated Capability need its own semantics beyond a Knowledge-depth overlay?

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN. [Desk alternatives and counterexamples](iteration-01-knowledge-space-and-applied-capability.md) are **not** user experiments or a selected model.
- **Question:** Can a user distinguish "I have enough coverage/depth on all listed topics" from "I have demonstrated their coordinated application for this particular professional task"?
- **Freeze before execution:** build one small stable Knowledge set (e.g. API, transactions, idempotency, retries), two distinct applied Capabilities (e.g. implement payment intent; explain distributed failure tradeoffs), and two Goals with different constraints. Include a user who can describe individual K's but has failed an integrated task, and a separate unobserved K (unknown, not zero). Describe one technically valid alternative approach with a different K-support set.
- **Compare:** H-A fixed K bundle-depth projection; H-B action/outcome/conditions with conditional K role relations; H-C independent scalar 'K application' depth. Same facts and task prompt; no exclusive hints.
- **Falsifiers:** required K lists imply universal necessity despite alternatives; full individual K coverage incorrectly predicts successful C; an unobserved K is shown as inability; a C assertion loses what activity was actually performed for which Goal; model merely renames execution step ordering as C parts.
- **Measure:** for each case, can one (i) identify the intended C performance, (ii) explain why these K's are selected *under these conditions*, (iii) distinguish what has been observed from what remains unknown, (iv) select a specific next learning or integrative-practice action? Mark logical representability separately from usefulness observed with people.
- **Stop:** compare outputs/counterexamples and report insufficient evidence honestly; do **not** write Harness Domain Model, grant relation authority, infer personal competence, or implement UI before needed.

**EX-PLD-05 research input (2026-10-12):** [Iteration 02](iteration-02-knowledge-skill-capability-reducibility.md) adds checked references for philosophical knowing-how, ACT/procedural learning, 4C/ID, EQF, competence-based knowledge spaces and evidence-centered assessment. It completes a **desk literature / synthetic-case check only**, **not** EX-PLD-05; retain **PROPOSED / NOT RUN**. In the later comparison explicitly distinguish a K topic's explanatory depth from a person's demonstrated performance on task conditions, and allow alternative K-support bundles for the same outcome.

## EX-PLD-06 — Goal → Capability → Knowledge → external theoretical study loop

- **Status:** PROPOSED / NOT RUN. Requires a real learner's goal/task and credible reviewed source content/material before a user-value trial. [Owner-aligned research framing](research-framing.md); [latest alternative comparison](iteration-05-owner-knowledge-scope-to-study-material-boundary.md). Earlier [Iterations 03](iteration-03-goal-capability-knowledge-external-retrieval-loop.md) and [04](iteration-04-goal-to-useful-action-market-and-scenarios.md) use **historical, conflicting variant letters**; their old names do not govern this protocol.
- **Question:** Does a goal- and Capability-scoped Knowledge view add incremental *orientation and selection value* and/or help produce suitable **theoretical study/retrieval material** for external use versus viable tools without Prep? Do **not** measure practical professional Capability or employability as the outcome.
- **Frozen fixture before execution:** one authentic preparation Goal plus source/constraints (e.g. one vacancy), two intelligible Capability Descriptions (allow learner to skip this lens where unhelpful), 8–12 reusable Knowledge topics with short explanations, relations, conditional relevance and explicit scope unknowns, plus 8–16 **independently reviewed** candidate study prompts including a known unsuitable prompt. Include one Knowledge item in two Capability scopes and differing Goal-relative learning objectives/depths. Do not assert a universal ordinal mastery scale. The preparatory editorial feasibility question belongs to [EX-PLD-07](open-experiments.md#ex-pld-07--editorial-feasibility-of-a-trustworthy-goal--knowledge-scope--practice-packet); a single author's self-review is not two independent reviewers.
- **Canonical comparison labels for the next experiment (not selected product options):**
  - **D — existing-tools baseline (no Prep):** the participant uses a credible current combination of source text, roadmaps/notes, AI/NotebookLM/RemNote and, where suitable, Anki or another external trainer. Not an artificial blank-sheet condition or an assumed worse workflow.
  - **C — study-material preparation without a Knowledge/Capability navigator:** from the same task/source material, make/select usable theoretical prompts and export where suitable. Tests whether card/material preparation alone captures most of the value.
  - **A — Knowledge orientation without prepared prompts:** a navigable Goal/Capability/Knowledge scope with relations, explicit uncertainty and intended learning objectives; participants can still use the same external tools to prepare study items afterward. Tests independent orientation value.
  - **B — orientation plus prepared study materials:** A's navigable Knowledge scope + selection of suitably reviewed items and one-way export, e.g. Anki-importable notes. Tests the incremental combined path.
- **Comparison controls:** same original Goal, source evidence, availability of external tools, support, and opportunity to correct errors; same subject facts and **independent review rubric** for all outputs. Do not give the baseline an artificially inferior corpus, but do not secretly provide it the finished candidate product either. Record authoring and quality-checking effort for each route; predefine treatment order/learning effects for any within-person comparison. Any synthetic vignette is labelled and is **not** counted as observed user-value evidence.
- **Primary observations, separately reported:** (1) *orientation*: correctness of the user's account of relevant/unknown scope, reasons for Knowledge relevance, relations and target learning objective versus an independently reviewed reference; (2) *material preparation*: accuracy, prompt-to-objective fit, reviewability, rejected unsuitable items, corrections/effort and successful import/first appropriate **theoretical** retrieval action where appropriate; (3) *incremental cost/benefit*: friction, time, manual edits, abandonment and expressed rationale relative to D and C. An A participant need not export anything to obtain orientation value. Export success/card count alone does not prove a useful study outcome.
- **Falsifiers:** A or B offers no credible improvement over D; C alone is comparable to B at much lower effort; Capability navigation adds confusion; topic completeness/necessity is overstated; target depth mismatches questions; inaccurate or redundant prompts consume review time; a user infers mastery of K or actual Capability from repetition.
- **Deferred add-on, not a fifth arm of this experiment:** user-authorized return of external review history/statistics and mapping to selected material. It had the label **P-C** in the dated Iteration 03 and **C** in Iteration 04; under this active comparison **C means material preparation only**. If later tested, specify separate evidence/identity/consent checks. Existing accepted general Activity/Result intake and history requirements are **not cancelled** by deferring automatic Anki statistics synchronization.
- **Stop/evidence:** report each arm's output, independent material/scope quality assessment, limitations and counterexamples, and an Owner choice packet. No automatic content-generation, domain taxonomy, Anki-specific integration, competence assessment or Harness approval follows from a passing desk/import check.

## EX-PLD-07 — Editorial feasibility of a trustworthy goal → knowledge scope → practice packet

- **Status:** ORIGINAL TWO-INDEPENDENT-REVIEWER EXPERIMENT NOT RUN. A bounded **single-author vacancy/source/packet desk preflight** was completed on 2026-10-12: [trace matrix, ten items, unsafe counterexamples and unmeasured review-cost assumptions](experiment-07-squire-editorial-feasibility-preflight.md). No reviewer agreement, independently validated material, timed editorial cost or learner outcomes. Motivated by the desk market and real vacancy comparison in [Iteration 04](iteration-04-goal-to-useful-action-market-and-scenarios.md), not an additional observed user study or an implementation commitment.
- **Question:** Can a useful and honest study scope and fit-for-objective practice packet be assembled from one *specific* ambiguous IT vacancy at a sustainable human-review cost, preserving unknowns and conditional Knowledge relevance?
- **Freeze before execution:** use the same publicly accessible vacancy text (e.g. SQUIRE payments-backend role, snapshot/date), two independently working subject reviewers, same goal definition, two applied outcome descriptions, ~10 relevant Knowledge topics, and ~10 prompts/tasks of mixed types. Record source assumptions; declare interviews/experience/production constraints beyond cards. Hold the input and review rubric fixed when comparing output.
- **Measure:** reviewer agreement/disagreement over topic necessity versus plausible relevance, unsupported claims, quality/fit of every practice item, estimated authoring/review/maintenance effort and which output can be used in Anki versus requires external applied task feedback.
- **Falsifiers:** experts cannot agree without inventing employer criteria; the 'complete' scope is misleading; too much editorial time per new goal; reliable recall cards fail to support the intended applied objective; a simpler manual/AI workflow is already sufficient.
- **Stop:** report quality and cost with counterexamples; no automatic adoption of authoring/source-verification Product Requirements or AI content pipeline. A synthetic desk matrix is **not** execution of this reviewer study.
